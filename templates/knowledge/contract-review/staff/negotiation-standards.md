# Negotiation standards

## Fallback ladder
Start with the approved position, then offer a narrower fallback only if the matter owner approves. Record red lines, concessions, and the reason for each concession in the matter note.

## Audit rights
For regulated or data-processing suppliers, retain audit rights or an independent assurance report. Escalate if the supplier refuses both.

## Data-processing liability
Data-processing liability should not be limited to nominal fees where breach impact could exceed the commercial value of the contract. Escalate caps below 12 months of fees.
