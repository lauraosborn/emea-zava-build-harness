# Contract clause playbook

## Liability cap
For customer-friendly positions, prefer a liability cap of at least 12 months of fees, with uncapped liability for confidentiality, data protection, IP infringement, fraud, wilful misconduct, and payment obligations. A 3-month fee cap is a high-risk supplier position for critical services.

## Auto-renewal
Auto-renewal should require written notice at least 30 days before renewal and should include a reminder obligation. Avoid terms that renew for more than one year without explicit approval.

## Indirect loss
Broad exclusion of all indirect loss can remove meaningful remedies. Check whether service credits, wasted expenditure, data restoration costs, and regulatory costs are expressly preserved.
