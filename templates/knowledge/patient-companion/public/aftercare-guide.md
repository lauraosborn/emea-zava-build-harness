# Clinician-approved aftercare guide

## Knee arthroscopy dressing
Keep the dressing dry for 48 hours unless the clinician gave different written instructions. After 48 hours, remove the outer dressing if it is clean and dry. Do not remove steri-strips unless advised by the clinic.

## Pain relief
Use paracetamol as directed on the discharge sheet. Avoid ibuprofen if the discharge sheet says to avoid anti-inflammatory medicines, or if the patient has been told not to take it. Contact the clinic or pharmacist if pain is not controlled.

## Red symptoms
Seek urgent clinical advice for fever, increasing redness, calf swelling, shortness of breath, chest pain, wound discharge, or pain that suddenly gets worse.

## Physiotherapy at home
Do the approved exercises slowly, stop if pain sharply increases, and record swelling, range of movement, and walking tolerance before the next appointment.
