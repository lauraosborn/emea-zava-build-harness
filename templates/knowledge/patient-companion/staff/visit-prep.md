# Visit preparation playbook

## Two-week follow-up
Ask about wound appearance, pain control, walking distance, swelling, exercise adherence, medication issues, and any red symptoms. Remind the patient to bring the discharge sheet and any photos they were asked to take.

## Wound check tracking
Record date, temperature if available, redness, swelling, discharge, dressing condition, pain score, and whether symptoms are improving, stable, or worsening.
