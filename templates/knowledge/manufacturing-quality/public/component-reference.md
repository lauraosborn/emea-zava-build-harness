# Component reference set

## A17 upper mount bracket
Reference image A17-REF-01 shows a uniform satin surface around the upper mounting point. A dull circular patch, crescent scoring, or radial scratch pattern within 12 mm of the upper mounting point is classified as a possible inconsistent surface finish defect. Hold the part for review when the affected area is larger than 6 mm or crosses a mounting face.

## B04 actuator bracket
Reference image B04-REF-02 permits light witness marks on the rear face only. Burrs around bolt holes, bright scratches on the machined face, or loose swarf are reject conditions until deburred and re-inspected.

## C22 control housing
Reference image C22-REF-03 permits minor colour variation on non-contact edges. Uneven coating on sealing faces or mounting points is a hold condition. If dimensions are within tolerance, route to coating inspection before scrap.

## D09 pump cover
Reference image D09-REF-04 requires a continuous bead and clean gasket channel. Missing bead sections, oil residue in the channel, or chatter marks near the sealing land require containment of the last ten parts.
