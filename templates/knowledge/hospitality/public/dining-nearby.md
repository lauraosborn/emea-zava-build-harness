# Places to eat nearby

## Nonna Bea's pizzeria
Family Italian, 6 minutes' walk along Quay Street. Certified nut-free kitchen, no nuts or nut oils on site. Children's menu and high chairs. Open 12:00 to 22:00. Good for lunch with children. Takes bookings for groups of 4 or more.

## The Salt Loft
Seafood and grills overlooking the harbour, 4 minutes' walk. Cooks with nuts in some desserts and sauces; staff can advise. Children welcome. Dinner 17:30 to 22:00. Book ahead at weekends.

## Copper Kettle Cafe
Cafe in the Market Hall, 8 minutes' walk, all indoors. Breakfasts, soups, sandwiches and cakes. Gluten-free and vegan options. Cakes may contain nuts; savoury menu is nut-free by recipe but not a nut-free kitchen. Open 08:00 to 17:00.

## Spice Quay
Indian restaurant, 10 minutes' walk. Many dishes contain cashews, almonds and peanuts; not recommended for guests with nut allergies. Open 17:00 to 23:00.

## Fishers Rest
Traditional fish and chips, 5 minutes' walk. Cooks in beef dripping; gluten-free batter on Tuesdays and Thursdays. Nut-free menu. Indoor seating for 40. Open 11:30 to 21:00.
