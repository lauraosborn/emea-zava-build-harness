# Hotel policies

## Check-in and checkout
Check-in is from 15:00. Checkout is by 11:00. Late checkout until 13:00 costs £25 and depends on availability; ask reception the evening before. Express checkout: leave your key card in the box at reception and your bill is emailed.

## Luggage storage after checkout
Free luggage storage at reception until 18:00 on the day of departure. Bags are tagged and kept in a locked room. Collect them from reception with your tag.

## Breakfast times
Breakfast is served in The Harbour Room restaurant on the ground floor. Monday to Friday 06:30 to 10:30. Saturday, Sunday and bank holidays 07:00 to 11:00. Children under 12 eat breakfast free with a paying adult. Grab-and-go breakfast bags are available from 05:30 if ordered by 22:00 the night before.

## Allergies and dietary needs
Tell your server about any allergy before ordering. The Harbour Room kitchen handles nuts, sesame, gluten, dairy and shellfish. A full allergen matrix is available for every dish. Nut-free breakfast options are prepared in a separate area on request; we cannot guarantee a completely nut-free environment. Gluten-free bread and plant-based milks are always available.

## Families and children
Cots and extra beds are free for children under 3 and must be booked in advance. Family rooms sleep up to four. Board games, children's books and colouring packs can be borrowed from reception. Babysitting can be arranged with 24 hours' notice.

## Wi-Fi, parking and pets
Free Wi-Fi network: ZavaHarbour-Guest, no password, accept the terms page. Parking in the hotel car park costs £15 per 24 hours; electric vehicle chargers are bays 1 to 4. Dogs are welcome in ground floor rooms for £20 per night.

## Quiet hours and smoking
Quiet hours are 23:00 to 07:00. All rooms and balconies are non-smoking; a £150 cleaning charge applies. The smoking area is by the car park entrance.
