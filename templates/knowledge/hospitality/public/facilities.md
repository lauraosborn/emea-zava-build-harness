# Hotel facilities

## Tide Spa opening hours
Tide Spa is on the lower ground floor and is open daily 07:00 to 21:00. Treatments must be booked at least 4 hours ahead at reception or on extension 2200. Robes and slippers are in your room. Treatments start from £45 for 30 minutes.

## Swimming pool and children's swim times
The indoor pool, sauna and steam room are part of Tide Spa. Children under 16 may use the pool only during family swim times: 09:00 to 11:00 and 15:00 to 17:00, with an adult. Sauna and steam room are for over 16s only. Lifeguard on duty during family swim times.

## Gym
The gym is open 24 hours with your room key card. Under 16s are not permitted.

## The Harbour Room restaurant
The hotel restaurant serves breakfast, lunch 12:00 to 14:30 and dinner 18:00 to 21:30. Children's menu available. Allergen matrix available for every dish. Booking recommended for dinner on Fridays and Saturdays.

## Lantern Bar
Lantern Bar is open 12:00 to 23:30 and serves light meals until 21:00. Under 18s welcome with an adult until 20:00.

## Games room and kids corner
The games room on the first floor has a pool table, table football, board games and a reading corner. Open 08:00 to 21:00. A good indoor option on rainy days for children.
