# Getting around Port Ellery

## Walking routes from the hotel
Turn left out of the main entrance onto Quay Street. The North Pier is 3 minutes ahead. The Maritime Museum is 7 minutes along Quay Street on the right, in the Customs House. The aquarium is at the far end of the harbour, 10 minutes. The Market Hall and Copper Kettle Cafe are up Church Steps from the museum, 8 minutes. Most harbour routes are flat; covered walkway along the Market Hall arcade on rainy days.

## Buses
Bus 7 stops outside the hotel every 20 minutes, 07:00 to 22:00, to the Climb Centre, Castle Ellery and the railway station. Single fare £2, children under 5 free. Pay by card on board.

## Taxis
The taxi rank is on Quay Street opposite the hotel. Reception can call Ellery Cabs; a 6-seater is available on request. Typical fare to the railway station is £9.

## Railway station
Port Ellery station is 12 minutes by bus 7 or 6 minutes by taxi. Trains to the city leave at 12 and 42 minutes past each hour.
