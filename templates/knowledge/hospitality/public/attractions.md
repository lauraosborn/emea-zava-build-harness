# Things to do in Port Ellery

## Port Ellery Aquarium (indoor, rainy day)
Indoor aquarium with a shark tunnel and rock pool touch tanks. 10 minutes' walk along the harbour. Open 10:00 to 17:00, last entry 16:00. Allow 1.5 to 2 hours. Family ticket for 2 adults and 2 children is £48; 10% off with a hotel key card. Cafe on site is nut-free.

## Maritime Museum (indoor, rainy day)
Free museum of shipwrecks, lighthouses and smuggling in the old Customs House, 7 minutes' walk. Open 09:30 to 16:30. Allow 1 hour. Children's trail and dressing-up corner.

## Ellery Glassworks (indoor, rainy day)
Watch glassblowing demonstrations and make your own glass bauble. 12 minutes' walk. Open 10:00 to 16:00, make-your-own sessions on the hour, 45 minutes, £12 per person, age 5 and over. Book at reception.

## Castle Ellery (outdoor)
Ruined clifftop castle with sea views, 20 minutes' walk uphill or bus 7. Open 09:00 to 18:00. Exposed and slippery in wet weather; not recommended on rainy days.

## Harbour boat trip (outdoor)
45 minute seal-spotting trip from the North Pier, 3 minutes' walk. Departures 10:30, 12:00 and 14:30, weather permitting. Cancelled in rain or strong wind.

## Clifftop Climb Centre (indoor)
Indoor climbing and soft play, 15 minutes by bus 7 or 6 minutes by taxi. Open 09:00 to 20:00. Climbing for age 6 and over.
