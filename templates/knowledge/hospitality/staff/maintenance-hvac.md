# Maintenance manual: room heating and air conditioning

## Air conditioning not working: first checks at reception
Most room air conditioning (AC) faults are not faults. Before escalating, ask the guest or check: 1. Is a key card in the power slot by the door? AC switches off without it. 2. Is the balcony door or window fully closed? A contact sensor disables AC when it is open. 3. Is the wall thermostat set to COOL or AUTO, not FAN or OFF, and below the room temperature? 4. Allow 15 minutes after changing settings; the unit has a compressor delay.

## Air conditioning thermostat reset
If the thermostat display is blank or frozen: switch the AC isolator in the wardrobe (labelled HVAC) off for 30 seconds and back on, then hold the FAN button for 10 seconds until the display flashes. Settings return to 21 degrees, AUTO. Reception may talk the guest through this by phone.

## Air conditioning escalation to maintenance
Escalate to the duty engineer (extension 4400, radio channel 2) if: the unit is leaking water, there is a burning smell or unusual noise, the room is above 26 degrees after the checks and reset, or the breaker trips again. Log a ticket in HotelOps with room number, checks done and time. Rooms 201 to 230 are served by air handling unit AHU-2 on the roof; if several second-floor rooms report AC faults, tell the engineer it may be AHU-2.

## While the guest waits
Offer a portable fan (store room B, key from duty manager) and complimentary drinks. If it cannot be fixed within 1 hour, offer a room move of the same category. Follow up with the guest within 30 minutes.

## Radiators and heating
Heating is controlled by the same thermostat set to HEAT or AUTO. Radiators in older rooms (101 to 120) have manual valves turned anticlockwise to open.
