# Maintenance manual: rooms and in-room technology

## Key card not working
Most key card failures are demagnetised cards from phones or bags. Re-encode the card at reception on the KeyWriter terminal: select the room, choose Duplicate, tap the card. If a re-encoded card still fails, the door lock battery may be low (lock light flashes amber three times): send the duty engineer with a spare battery pack and the emergency override key from the duty manager safe. Always check photo ID or the booking name before re-encoding a card.

## TV no signal
Press SOURCE on the remote and choose HDMI 1. If the screen says no signal on HDMI 1, unplug the set-top box behind the TV for 20 seconds. Escalate to the duty engineer if two or more rooms on the same floor have no signal.

## No hot water
Run the tap for 2 minutes; upper floors take longer. If still cold, check whether other rooms on the floor are affected and call the duty engineer on extension 4400. Do not reset boilers; only engineers may do this.

## Smoke alarm beeping
A single chirp every minute means a low battery: send maintenance to replace it, do not let guests remove the alarm. A continuous alarm means follow the fire procedure.

## Wi-Fi problems in a room
Ask the guest to forget the ZavaHarbour-Guest network and reconnect, then open a browser to see the terms page. If several rooms report problems, restart the floor access point from the HotelOps network page or call IT support on extension 4600.
