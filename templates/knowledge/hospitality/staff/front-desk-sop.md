# Front desk standard operating procedures

## Handling an upset guest
Use LAST: Listen without interrupting, Apologise sincerely, Solve or explain the next step and when, Thank the guest. Do not argue about fault. Write it up in HotelOps before the end of your shift.

## Compensation authority
Front desk staff may offer up to £30 of food and drink credit or a free late checkout without a manager. Room moves within the same category are allowed. Refunds, upgrades or anything above £30 need the duty manager (extension 1000).

## Guest privacy and data protection
Never confirm whether a person is staying at the hotel, and never give out a room number, to anyone, including callers claiming to be family or colleagues. Offer to take a message and pass it on. Never say a room number aloud at the desk; write it down. Guest data must only be viewed in HotelOps for a business reason.

## Allergen requests
Record allergies on the guest profile in HotelOps and tell the restaurant manager. Never promise a guest that a dish or venue is allergen-free; refer them to the allergen matrix and the chef. For severe allergies, note where the nearest defibrillator is (lobby, next to the lifts) and the first aiders on shift.

## Medical emergency
Call 999 first, then the duty manager. Send a first aider. Keep the lobby clear for paramedics and send someone to meet them at the main entrance.

## Lost property
Log every item in the lost property book with date, place found and finder. Valuables go in the duty manager safe. Items are kept for 3 months. Only return items after checking ID against the booking.
